Privacy Policy

Maska Piano · Effective date: October 28, 2026

In short

Maska Piano does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to sign in or create an account.
  • It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, identifiers or usage statistics.
  • It does not contain advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or any other third-party software that collects data.
  • It does not need an internet connection and does not send anything to any server.

What is stored on your device

To remember your music, the app keeps data only on your iPhone: your settings (such as the instrument, the hints and the room), the pieces you record and keep, and the last minutes you played. This is stored in the app's own storage on the device. It never leaves your device through the app. It is removed when you delete the app. If you use iCloud or computer backups for your device, Apple may include this app data in your device backup under your own Apple account; the developer has no access to it.

Sharing and opening files

If you choose to share a piece as a sound or a MIDI file, the app opens the standard iOS share sheet. What you send, and to whom, is your decision; the app itself transmits nothing. A MIDI file you open in the app is read on your device and kept with your pieces.

Third parties

None. No third-party services receive any information from the app.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children, and contains no ads. Links leave the app only when tapped: “Privacy” opens this page in Safari, and “Write to us” opens your own mail app addressed to us; nothing is sent unless you send it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Any change that involved collecting data would first require an app update that you could choose not to install.
