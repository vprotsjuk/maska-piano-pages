How to play

  1. Turn the iPhone sideways and play the keys with your fingers.
  2. As you play, the piano hears which key your music is in and lights its notes. Notes outside it stay dark but still play.
  3. The mask button chooses how much help you get, from a full hint to none.
  4. The left wheel is the pedal, the right wheel the volume. Swipe or pinch the small keyboard at the top to move along the keys.
  5. Tap ● to record, and ▶ to open your pieces and the built-in ones. A piece plays back on the keys.
  6. Share a piece as a sound file or a MIDI file; MIDI files sent to you open in the app.

FAQ

I hear no sound.

Raise the volume with the side buttons and check the right wheel in the app. If headphones or a speaker are connected, the sound goes there.

The sound comes late in my headphones.

Bluetooth headphones add a delay of their own, often about a quarter of a second. Wired headphones or the phone's speaker play at once.

Are my pieces saved?

Yes, on your device. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account, so pieces do not move to another device by themselves. Deleting the app deletes them; share the ones you want to keep first.

Does it need internet?

No. The whole piano works offline.

Does it collect any data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
